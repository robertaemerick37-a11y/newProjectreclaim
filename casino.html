Casino Scams

What Are Casino Scams?

Casino scams involve fraudulent schemes that target users of online gambling platforms. Scammers often create fake casino websites or manipulate games to deceive victims into depositing money.

These scams may appear legitimate at first, offering attractive bonuses or showing false winnings to build trust before restricting withdrawals or demanding additional payments.

Common Types of Casino Scams

Fake Online Casinos

Fraudsters operate websites that mimic legitimate casinos but are designed solely to collect deposits without allowing real withdrawals.

Rigged Games

Some platforms manipulate game outcomes to ensure players lose once larger amounts are deposited.

Withdrawal Restrictions

Victims may be required to meet unrealistic conditions, pay unexpected fees, or provide unnecessary verification before accessing their funds.

Bonus Traps

Attractive bonus offers are used to encourage deposits but often include hidden terms that prevent users from withdrawing winnings.

Impersonation of Legitimate Casinos

Scammers may imitate trusted brands or use fake support agents to convince victims their accounts or winnings require additional payments.
